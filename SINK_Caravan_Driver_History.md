### Caravan history instructions for AI assistants

- For SINK selection history, **exclude** 12 October PLKM / FearlesS~KiNG. Do not infer they represented SINK. Their full name was verified from the 13 October player-detail screenshot, even though a later history list truncated it.
- For future SINK caravan driver recommendations, compare candidates against **all dated SINK records** above, prioritize eligible players not previously selected or not selected recently, and avoid repetitive selections. Do not automatically reselect the same driver; do not claim historical drivers are permanently banned unless the user says so.
- These are observed historical drivers, **not** a current driver roster or a guaranteed complete lifetime history. A lack of recorded history does not prove someone has never driven.
- Use exact player-name spellings as displayed, including Cyrillic **БОНИ**, **äxel**, and **Jaxel**. Do not silently assume Jaxel and äxel refer to the same player; flag any suspected account rename for verification.
- Do not conflate history timestamps, levels or might with Alliance Duel scores. More recent history screenshots may show changed might values for the same dated driver.
- When a new Alliance Stable screenshot is supplied, **upsert by date + alliance + driver**, preserve existing older dates, log conflicting might values rather than counting the same dated driver twice, and update the history consistently in this master and any single-file AI data export.
